Inside the platform

Everything your safety file needs

Health & Safety

Keep people competent and protected — then prove it.

  • Training certificates, expiry tracking, gap analysis
  • Occupational medicals and compliance reporting
  • PPE stock, size lists, issue register, e-sign
  • Safety committee elections, meetings, actions
  • Rule-based risk assessments from HSE templates
  • Hazardous chemicals + SDS uploads
  • Emergency drill logging

Compliance

Turn obligations into live registers your team can work from.

  • Legal registers and legislation references
  • 277-item HSE audit checklist with evidence
  • Formal H&S appointments with dual e-sign
  • Daily / weekly / monthly inspections
  • Non-conformance reports
  • Incident investigation and cost analysis

Site Safety

Control who is on site and what high-risk work is allowed.

  • Toolbox talks with attendee signatures
  • Induction training and expiry tracking
  • Visitor register with host and purpose
  • Permit to work (hot work, height, confined space)
  • Maintenance schedules for plant and vehicles

Contractor Management

Hold contractors to the same compliance standard.

  • Safety-file portal with secure upload links
  • Scored compliance across OHS sections
  • Document library for procedures and certificates
  • Clear view of who is current — and who is not
