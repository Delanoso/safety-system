Inside the platform

Everything your safety file needs

Health & Safety

Training & gap analysis, medicals, PPE stock/issue with e-sign, SHE committee elections & meetings, rule-based risk assessments, hazardous chemicals with SDS, emergency drills.

Compliance

SA legal registers, 277-item HSE audit checklist, formal legal appointments (Section 16, SHE reps), daily/weekly/monthly inspections with NCRs, incident investigation & cost analysis.

Site Safety

Toolbox talks with attendee signatures, induction training, visitor check-in, and permit to work for hot work, height, confined space, and related tasks.

Contractors & Docs

Contractor safety-file portal with token uploads and scored compliance, plus a structured document library for procedures, certificates, and company files.
